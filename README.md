🌳 FINANCE LEGACY
AI-Powered Forgotten Financial Asset Discovery & Recovery Platform
Recover What Matters.

FINANCE LEGACY is an AI-powered fintech platform designed to help individuals and families discover, organize, verify, and recover potentially forgotten financial assets. By analyzing financial documents, employment records, and transaction clues, the platform transforms scattered financial information into actionable recovery insights.

🚨 Problem Statement
Families often lose track of financial assets such as:

Bank accounts
Fixed deposits
Employee Provident Fund (EPF)
Insurance policies
Pension accounts
Mutual funds and investments
Government savings schemes
Financial information is scattered across documents, institutions, and records. When a family member passes away, nominees often struggle to identify assets, organize documents, understand claim procedures, and navigate recovery processes.

This results in forgotten assets, delayed claims, financial loss, and unnecessary difficulties for families.

💡 Our Solution
FINANCE LEGACY provides a centralized AI-powered platform that analyzes financial documents, identifies potential forgotten assets, detects missing financial connections, and guides users through the recovery process.

The platform combines AI document intelligence, financial footprint analysis, asset discovery, recovery guidance, claim tracking, and nominee access into one unified ecosystem.

⚠️ Disclaimer
FINANCE LEGACY is an informational and financial document intelligence platform.

Potential assets identified by AI are clues and should not be considered confirmed financial holdings. Users must verify assets through the respective financial institutions and official channels.

The prototype does not directly access private financial databases or automatically submit official claims.

Demo data is fictional and intended only for demonstration purposes.
